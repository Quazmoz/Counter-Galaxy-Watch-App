# Tally Counter App for Wear OS Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

Canonical public policy URL: https://consultant.quinnfavo.com/privacy/tally-counter

Tally Counter App for Wear OS is a simple watch counter utility.

The app may store counter values, app settings, and basic local state on your watch or paired device. This data is used only to run the counter features.

The developer does not intentionally collect, receive, sell, or share personal information from in-app use. The app does not require an account, developer backend, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or cloud sync.

You can delete local data by resetting the counter where available, clearing app storage in Android/Wear OS settings, or uninstalling the app.

For privacy questions, contact Quazmoz@vivaldi.net.
